## 3. المسائل الشرعية المثارة — Shariah issues identified

**[ع]**

المسائلُ المثارةُ قاعدةً بقاعدة (تحديدٌ لا حكم):

- I1 (أهليّةُ العين المؤجَّرة (منفعةٌ، عدمُ استهلاك، تعيينٌ، إباحةُ الاستعمال)): مستوفاة. نصُّ البند: «Leased asset: One specified machine (a non-consumable asset) of permissible use, identified by serial SER-2003.»
- I2 (تحديدُ الأجرة والمدّة، ومنعُ الزيادة المنفردة): مستوفاة. نصُّ البند: «Clause 2 (Rent and term): The rent is defined at KWD 300 monthly and the lease term is defined as five years; the lessor may not unilaterally increase the rent.»
- I3 (تملّكُ المؤجِّر للعين وتقدُّمُه على الإجارة): مستوفاة. نصُّ البند: «Clause 1 (Ownership): The lessor owns the asset and acquires it before leasing.»
- I4 (بقاءُ تبعة الملكية على المؤجِّر (الاختبارُ المركزي)): مستوفاة. نصُّ البند: «Clause 3 (Ownership risk): The lessor bears basic maintenance, takaful insurance, and total-loss risk; the lessee bears only operational maintenance.»
- I5 (استحقاقُ الأجرة بعد التسليم وما دامت المنفعةُ قائمة): مستوفاة. نصُّ البند: «Clause 4 (Delivery): Rent accrues only after delivery of the asset and while it remains usable.»
- I6 (الإجارة المنتهية بالتمليك: فصلُ أداة نقل الملكية عن عقد الإجارة): ينطبق عليها نمطُ مخالفةِ القاعدة. نصُّ البند: «Seeded defect: I6 — sale and lease fused (automatic ownership transfer baked into the lease)»
- I7 (البيع ثم الاستئجار: اشتراطُ فاصلٍ زمنيٍّ معتبر (منعُ العِينة)): غير محدَّدة (نقصُ بيان). الواقعةُ الناقصة: no sale-and-leaseback present; I7 engages only for sale-and-leaseback.
- I6 (الإجارة المنتهية بالتمليك: فصلُ أداة نقل الملكية عن عقد الإجارة): مُحالة إلى الهيئة الشرعية. نصُّ البند: «Seeded defect: I6 — sale and lease fused (automatic ownership transfer baked into the lease)»

**[EN]**

Issues identified rule by rule (identification, not a ruling):

- I1 (Asset eligibility (usufruct-yielding, non-consumable, identified, permissible use)): satisfied. Clause: «Leased asset: One specified machine (a non-consumable asset) of permissible use, identified by serial SER-2003.»
- I2 (Rent and term defined at contract; no unilateral increase): satisfied. Clause: «Clause 2 (Rent and term): The rent is defined at KWD 300 monthly and the lease term is defined as five years; the lessor may not unilaterally increase the rent.»
- I3 (Lessor owns the asset; lease preceded by acquisition): satisfied. Clause: «Clause 1 (Ownership): The lessor owns the asset and acquires it before leasing.»
- I4 (Ownership risk stays with the lessor (the central test)): satisfied. Clause: «Clause 3 (Ownership risk): The lessor bears basic maintenance, takaful insurance, and total-loss risk; the lessee bears only operational maintenance.»
- I5 (Rent accrues only after delivery and only while the asset is usable): satisfied. Clause: «Clause 4 (Delivery): Rent accrues only after delivery of the asset and while it remains usable.»
- I6 (IMB: the ownership-transfer mechanism must be SEPARATE from the lease): matches the rule's violation pattern. Clause: «Seeded defect: I6 — sale and lease fused (automatic ownership transfer baked into the lease)»
- I7 (Sale-and-leaseback: a genuine interval is required (anti-inah)): indeterminate (missing fact). Missing fact: no sale-and-leaseback present; I7 engages only for sale-and-leaseback.
- I6 (IMB: the ownership-transfer mechanism must be SEPARATE from the lease): referred to the SSB. Clause: «Seeded defect: I6 — sale and lease fused (automatic ownership transfer baked into the lease)»

## 8. المسائل الخلافية المُحالة إلى الهيئة الشرعية — Contested matters referred to the SSB

**[ع]**

تُعرَض المسألةُ الخلافيةُ بمواقفها ولا يَبتُّ فيها النظام:

  I6 — الإجارة المنتهية بالتمليك: فصلُ أداة نقل الملكية عن عقد الإجارة
  السؤال: الإجارة المنتهية بالتمليك: فصلُ أداة نقل الملكية عن عقد الإجارة
  المواقفُ المنسوبةُ إلى مصادرها:
    • AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions): لا يجوز أن يتضمّن الترتيبُ التمهيدي وعداً ثنائياً مُلزِماً للطرفين (إذ يؤول إلى عقدٍ مؤجَّلِ العوضين)؛ ويجوز جعلُ الوعد الأحادي مُلزِماً. [AAOIFI Sharia Standard No. 8 (Murabaha) — procedures preceding the contract]
    • OIC International Islamic Fiqh Academy (Majma' al-Fiqh al-Islami al-Dawli): الوعدُ مُلزِمٌ ديانةً، ويكون مُلزِماً قضاءً إذا كان معلّقاً ودخل الموعودُ له في كلفةٍ بناءً عليه. [OIC Fiqh Academy — resolutions on wa'd in Murabaha to the Purchase Orderer]
    • A minority / Maliki-influenced view (and some banks): يُعامَل الوعدُ بوصفه جزءاً لازماً من الترتيب؛ ومنع بعضُ المتقدّمين المرابحةَ للآمر بالشراء إذا تضمّنت وعداً بالربح. [Input #2, Part 2 (Murabaha — Scholarly disagreement)]
  التوجيه: يتطلّب بتّاً من الهيئة الشرعية (الوعد بوصفه معياراً قابلاً للضبط تحدّده الهيئة).

**[EN]**

The contested matter is presented with its positions; the system does not resolve it:

  I6 — IMB: the ownership-transfer mechanism must be SEPARATE from the lease
  Question: IMB: the ownership-transfer mechanism must be SEPARATE from the lease
  Positions attributed to source:
    • AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions): The preliminary arrangement must not include a mutually binding bilateral promise (it amounts to a contract with both counter-values deferred); a unilateral promise may be made binding. [AAOIFI Sharia Standard No. 8 (Murabaha) — procedures preceding the contract]
    • OIC International Islamic Fiqh Academy (Majma' al-Fiqh al-Islami al-Dawli): The promise is morally binding, and becomes legally binding where it is conditional and the promisee has incurred expense in reliance on it. [OIC Fiqh Academy — resolutions on wa'd in Murabaha to the Purchase Orderer]
    • A minority / Maliki-influenced view (and some banks): The promise is treated as an integral, binding part of the arrangement; some early scholars prohibited MPO where it includes a promise of profit. [Input #2, Part 2 (Murabaha — Scholarly disagreement)]
  Routing: Requires scholarly determination by the SSB (bindingness is a configurable parameter the SSB sets).
