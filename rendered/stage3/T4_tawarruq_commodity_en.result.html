Positions surfaced (not adjudicated):
  • OIC International Islamic Fiqh Academy: Ruled both organized and reverse tawarruq impermissible; the simultaneous, pre-arranged transactions between financier and mustawriq against a financial obligation are a deception containing riba. [OIC Fiqh Academy Resolution 179 (19/5), 19th session, Sharjah, UAE, 26–30 April 2009]
  • AAOIFI: Permits tawarruq subject to strict conditions: sale to a third party (not back to the original seller), and restriction of the bank's agency role. [AAOIFI Sharia Standard No. 30 (Monetization / Tawarruq); related SS 20, SS 18]
  • Malaysia — Bank Negara Shariah Advisory Council (SAC): Broadly permits organized tawarruq, even allowing agency (wakalah) arrangements that AAOIFI restricts — a clear GCC vs. Malaysia divergence. [Input #2, Part 2 (Tawarruq — Scholarly disagreement)]
  • Indonesia — DSN-MUI: Treats organized tawarruq (tawarruq munazzam) as problematic. [DSN-MUI Fatwa 82/2011 (per Input #2, Part 2)]
