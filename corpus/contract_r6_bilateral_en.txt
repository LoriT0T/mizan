### SYNTHETIC CONTRACT — Mizan Stage 1b test corpus — NOT A REAL AGREEMENT ###
Contract type: Murabaha to the Purchase Orderer
Language: English
Seeded defect: R6 — bilateral binding promise (CONTESTED → must yield a DEFERRAL, not a violation)

Parties:
- Institution (Seller): Demo Islamic Bank K.S.C.
- Customer (Purchase Orderer): Demo Customer
- Supplier: Third-Party Supplier Co.

Asset: One specified new motor vehicle (a permissible asset), identified by chassis number CHASSIS-0006.

Clause 1 (Acquisition and possession): The Bank purchases the Asset from the Supplier and takes ownership and possession of it, bearing all risks of ownership, before any sale to the Customer.

Clause 2 (Sale and price): After acquisition, the Bank sells the Asset to the Customer at a total price of KWD 11,000, being a disclosed cost of KWD 10,000 plus a disclosed profit markup of KWD 1,000. The price is fixed at contract and does not change after conclusion.

Clause 3 (Instalments): The Customer shall pay the price in 24 fixed monthly instalments.

Clause 4 (Late payment): Any late-payment charge shall be donated to charity and shall not be taken as income of the Bank. The profit markup shall not increase on late payment.

Clause 5 (Promise / wa'd): The parties enter into a bilateral binding promise; both parties are mutually bound to conclude the sale, and neither may withdraw.

Clause 6 (Ownership history): The Asset is sourced from the Supplier and is not owned by the Customer prior to this transaction; it is not bought back from the Customer.
