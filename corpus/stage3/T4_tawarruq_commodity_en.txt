Recitals:
The Customer requires personal cash financing. The parties have agreed a commodity tawarruq (monetization) arrangement as follows:

Parties:
- First Party (the Bank): a demonstration Islamic bank.
- Second Party (the Customer / mustawriq).

Clause 1 (Commodity purchase on deferred terms): The Bank sells to the Customer a specified quantity of a base metal commodity, held on an approved commodity platform, on deferred payment at a price equal to cost plus a disclosed profit (the Deferred Price), payable in instalments.

Clause 2 (Agency to on-sell for cash): The Customer appoints the Bank as agent to sell the same commodity to a third party for immediate spot cash, and the spot proceeds are credited to the Customer's account.

Clause 3 (Obligation): The Customer owes the Bank the Deferred Price over the financing term. The commodity is incidental; the economic result is cash now against a larger deferred obligation.

Clause 4 (Acknowledgement): This is an organized tawarruq facility arranged end-to-end by the Bank.
