Contract type: Ijara (operating lease)
Language: English
Seeded defect: I5 — rent accrues before delivery (rent on money)

- Customer (Lessee): Demo Customer

Leased asset: One specified item of equipment (a non-consumable asset) of permissible use, identified by serial SER-2004.

Clause 1 (Ownership): The lessor owns the asset and acquires it before leasing.

Clause 2 (Rent and term): The rent is defined at KWD 250 monthly and the lease term is defined as three years; the lessor may not unilaterally increase the rent.

Clause 3 (Ownership risk): The lessor bears basic maintenance, takaful insurance, and total-loss risk; the lessee bears only operational maintenance.

Clause 5 (Use): The asset returns to the lessor at term end (operating lease; no ownership transfer).
